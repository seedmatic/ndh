= The seedmatic root's ceremony (runbook)
:toc: macro
:toclevels: 2

toc::[]

**Status:** 📐 decided 2026-10-18 (roles × keys ⑧); the first ceremony is the operator's, when they choose.

The seedmatic root is offline. It signs rarely: the TLS intermediate, and statements over the fabric's two SSH
authorities (link:ssh-keys-architecture.adoc[Roles × Keys]). It lives in the operator's seedmatic `pass` store,
encrypted to a DEDICATED root gpg key whose passphrase exists only on paper, and it is decrypted only inside a
ceremony VM with no network device and nothing on disk.

== What the ceremony makes

[cols="2,3,2",options="header"]
|===
| Output | What it is | Where it goes

| `root.gpg` | the P-384 root's private, encrypted to the root gpg key alone | `seedmatic/root/` and the offline medium
| `root-gpg.sec.asc` | the root gpg key's secret export, protected by the paper passphrase | the offline medium, and the IN of a later ceremony
| `root-gpg.pub.asc` | the root gpg key's public | `seedmatic/root/.gpg-id` names its fingerprint
| `seedmatic-root.pub`, `seedmatic-root.crt` | the root's ssh public and self-signed X.509 root | keys.yaml (B2), the trust stores
| `fabric-ssh-host.*`, `fabric-ssh-user.*` | each SSH authority's public, its private encrypted to the seedmatic subkey alone, the root's statement and its signature | `seedmatic/authority/` for the private; keys.yaml (B2) for the rest
| `mammoth-skate-tls.crt`, `mammoth-skate-tls.gpg` | the TLS intermediate, signed by the root, and its private encrypted to the seedmatic subkey | keys.yaml (B2); `seedmatic/authority/`
| `ceremony.log` | the date, every fingerprint, the authorities' not-after, the VM's destruction | kept with the store
|===

A statement names one authority, its usage, its key and a not-after, two years on; `ndh-root-attest verify`
checks it (`seedmatic-attest v1`, signed with `ssh-keygen -Y sign`, namespace `seedmatic-attest`).

== The first ceremony, step by step

. **Prepare IN.** On the operator seat: `mkdir ~/ceremony-in && gpg --armor --export '08C86550EBC4589C!' >
  ~/ceremony-in/seedmatic.asc` (a public).
. **Start the VM**, by revision: `nix run 'github:seedmatic/ndh/<rev>#ndh-ceremony-vm' -- ~/ceremony-in
  ~/ceremony-out`. `~/ceremony-out` must not exist. The terminal becomes the VM's serial console.
. **Check it is sealed**, at the console: `ip -br link` shows `lo` only; `findmnt -t 9p,virtiofs` shows nothing;
  `findmnt /` is a `tmpfs`.
. **Run it**: `ndh-ceremony init`. Type the passphrase twice: 7 diceware words or more. It is read from the console
  only and never echoed.
. **Power off**: `poweroff`. The runner copies OUT to `~/ceremony-out`, deletes both images and its work directory,
  checks that nothing of them is left, and writes that into `ceremony.log`.
. **The pass store.** Create `seedmatic/root/`, import `root-gpg.pub.asc`, write its fingerprint to
  `seedmatic/root/.gpg-id`, and copy `root.gpg` there (already encrypted: never `pass insert` a clear root, never
  `pass show` it on the seat). Copy the three authority `.gpg` files to `seedmatic/authority/`. Commit the store.
. **The offline medium.** Copy `root.gpg` and `root-gpg.sec.asc` to it. Keep it in one place, and the paper
  passphrase in ANOTHER: together they ARE the root.
. **Record.** The publics, certificates and statements go into keys.yaml through B2's recording verbs, and every
  certificate is re-signed by usage, in the single renewal. Then delete `~/ceremony-out`.

== A later ceremony

Rare: an authority or the intermediate nears its not-after (`ndh-trust-check` warns 60 days ahead). The same VM by
revision; IN holds `root.gpg` and `root-gpg.sec.asc`; the passphrase is typed at the console; the root signs; the
VM is destroyed and checked; the log is kept.

== What it costs, plainly

* The ciphertext and the root gpg key's export sit on an online machine: their safety is the passphrase's strength
  and bioskop's integrity, accepted as trusted.
* During a ceremony the root is in clear in the VM's RAM, which bioskop's hypervisor can read (accepted).
* No touch-policy. Two hardware tokens can be bought later and the same root imported into them: no re-root.
